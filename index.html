In the depths of a neon-soaked mire, 144 cyberpunk warriors emerged — steel-veined predators bound to code, territory, and endless conflict.

The mire fractured into six hostile territories. Each territory is ruled by one Commander and defended by 24 warriors.

144 warriors. 6 Commanders. One living mire.

Commander: KRAZIX THE BLOOD-CODED
