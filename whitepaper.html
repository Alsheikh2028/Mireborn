01Abstract

NOXGATOR is a community-driven Web3 project on Solana. It fuses cyberpunk crocodile mythology with digital collectibles, a token, a daily check-in program, a 90-day SOL contribution ranking and a planned game. The universe also stands for awareness, preservation and respect for the crocodile, an endangered species.

Born from the swamp. Built to rule.

02The Mire and Its Rulers

In a neon-soaked mire, 144 cyberpunk warriors emerged: steel-veined predators bound to code, territory and endless conflict. The mire fractured into six hostile territories. Each has one Commander and 24 warriors. Alliances rot, power shifts, and the mire lives on war.

07Anti-Cheat and Fair Play

Rewards belong to real participants. The planned system covers:

  • One points claim per wallet per UTC day.
  • Wallet behavior checks and detection of linked fake wallets.
  • Activity reviews before rewards are distributed.
  • Removal of points and bans for confirmed cheating or reward manipulation.
