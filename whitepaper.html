01Abstract

NOXGATOR is a community-driven Web3 project on Solana. It fuses cyberpunk crocodile mythology with digital collectibles, a token, a 120-day points program, a Player Access Plan for early game access, a 90-day SOL contribution ranking and a planned game. The universe also stands for awareness, preservation and respect for the crocodile, an endangered species.

Born from the swamp. Built to rule.

10Wildlife Guardians

NOXGATOR is inspired by the crocodile ecosystem and supports awareness, preservation and respect for this endangered species. The Wildlife Guardians initiative shares reliable, species-specific information, points supporters to established conservation organisations, and publishes verified results only when real activities and records exist.

TRANSPARENCY: NOXGATOR does not collect or process donations. Donation links lead to external organisations. The Guardian quiz badges are stored only in your browser, have no monetary value, and are not connected to official points, whitelist, NFT or token systems. NOXGATOR does not claim a conservation partnership unless independently verified.
