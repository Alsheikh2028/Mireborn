1Introduction — the beginning of a new generation

NOXGATOR is developing an evolving gaming ecosystem built around an original swamp universe, characters, missions, territories, digital collectibles, and community participation.

The Great Egg Drop introduces a new collection into that universe: 5,000 NOX Eggs. The objective is to broaden participation, welcome new collectors and players, and create a foundation for carefully planned future connections between NFT ownership and gameplay.

This release is an ecosystem expansion initiative. It is not a replacement for the existing Genesis collection, and it does not change the separate 120-day points system.

2The Great Egg Drop — 5,000 NOX Eggs

The NOX EGG collection is planned as a fixed-supply release of up to 5,000 NFTs on Solana. The mint is planned for January 3, 2027, in one public stage open to everyone, without a whitelist phase or presale stage.

Each eligible wallet will be limited to one NOX EGG. The collection will be minted at a price of 0 SOL, with participants paying the network transaction fees required by Solana.

The public mint will remain available until the 5,000-NFT supply is minted or until the project announces otherwise through its official channels. The final start time, mint interface, verified collection address, and technical instructions will be published before launch.

3Genesis 150 — the original founders

Genesis 150 represents the original founding generation of NOXGATOR. Its limited supply and founding identity are separate from the NOX EGG collection.

Genesis status is intended to remain exclusive to the original Genesis collection. NOX EGG ownership does not confer Genesis Founder status, does not automatically qualify a holder for a Genesis NFT, and does not grant Genesis-exclusive benefits.

Genesis-related recognition, missions, and benefits remain governed by the separate Genesis rules and by features that are actually implemented and announced by the project.

4Two generations — two identities

NOXGATOR distinguishes the collections by their role in the ecosystem. Genesis 150 represents the founding generation and the original foundation of the swamp. NOX EGG represents a new generation of participants and collectors entering through the Egg Drop.

Genesis 150

  • Original founding generation
  • Foundation of the swamp
  • Supply unchanged: 150

NOX EGG

  • New generation of participants
  • Enters through the Egg Drop
  • Fixed supply: 5,000

The collections may coexist in the same broader ecosystem, but they do not share the same status. The 5,000 NOX Eggs do not dilute the numerical supply of Genesis 150, and the Egg Drop does not create additional Genesis NFTs.

5The NOX EGG concept — something is awakening

Within the NOXGATOR universe, an Egg represents the possibility of a future character and a new chapter in the swamp’s story. The project’s long-term development vision may explore ways to connect NOX Eggs with the game.

Potential directions include a distinct Egg Holder identity, community events, special missions, future in-game integration, and an egg-hatching mechanic that could connect Eggs to characters or other gameplay elements.

6The 120-day point system — a separate Genesis path

NOXGATOR’s existing 120-day points system is designed to recognize participation and provide a structured path toward the Genesis NFT mint opportunity, according to its published rules.

The Great Egg Drop is independent of that system. The Egg Drop does not shorten, reset, replace, or otherwise modify the 120-day period. Existing point accumulation and Genesis eligibility rules continue separately.

NOX EGG ownership does not automatically generate points, grant a higher ranking, or guarantee Genesis eligibility. Participation in the points system remains subject to its own rules.

7Community & ecosystem expansion

The Egg Drop is intended to widen the community around the game and introduce more people to the NOXGATOR universe. The project’s broader objective is to connect collectibles with meaningful participation, exploration, and gameplay as development progresses.

Community growth, events, missions, and future integrations will depend on development capacity, technical feasibility, and announcements made by the project. The collection itself should not be interpreted as a promise of financial returns or guaranteed future rewards.

8Public mint & fair distribution

The planned distribution model is straightforward: one public mint stage, open to everyone, with a maximum of one NOX EGG per wallet and a total collection supply capped at 5,000.

There will be no whitelist stage or presale stage in the planned format. The mint price is 0 SOL; participants pay applicable Solana network fees.

The project intends to use suitable technical controls to enforce the wallet limit and monitor the supply. A wallet limit is a technical rule, not proof of one-human-one-mint: a person may control multiple wallets. The project will publish the final rules and any anti-abuse measures before the mint.

9Transparency & security

Official mint details, the mint website, and the verified collection address will be communicated through NOXGATOR’s official channels. Participants should verify the domain and collection address before connecting a wallet or approving a transaction.

NEVER SHARE YOUR KEYSNOXGATOR will never require a participant’s private key or wallet recovery phrase. Users should not share these details with anyone, including accounts claiming to provide mint support.

On-chain details such as the collection address and minted supply should be independently verifiable once published. The project should clearly identify official links and warn the community about impersonation attempts.

11Long-term vision — one swamp, multiple generations

NOXGATOR’s long-term vision is an interconnected digital world in which different generations of participants have distinct identities and roles. Genesis 150 represents the original foundation. NOX EGG introduces a new generation.

Over time, the project aims to develop a richer gaming experience through characters, missions, territories, events, and community participation. The timing and scope of these developments will depend on implementation and will be communicated as milestones are confirmed.
