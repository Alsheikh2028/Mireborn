01Abstract

NOXGATOR is a community-driven Web3 project on Solana. It fuses cyberpunk crocodile mythology with digital collectibles, a token, a 120-day points program, a Player Access Plan for early game access, a 90-day SOL contribution ranking, The Great Egg Drop (a free 5,000-NFT mint that opens a second generation) and a planned game. The universe also stands for awareness, preservation and respect for the crocodile, an endangered species.

Born from the swamp. Built to rule.

06Genesis 150 and NOX EGG

GENESIS 150: THE ORIGINAL FOUNDERS

Genesis 150 represents the original founding generation of NOXGATOR. Its limited supply and founding identity are separate from the NOX EGG collection.

Genesis status is intended to remain exclusive to the original Genesis collection. NOX EGG ownership does not confer Genesis Founder status, does not automatically qualify a holder for a Genesis NFT, and does not grant Genesis-exclusive benefits.

Genesis-related recognition, missions, and benefits remain governed by the separate Genesis rules and by features that are actually implemented and announced by the project.

TWO GENERATIONS, TWO IDENTITIES

NOXGATOR distinguishes the collections by their role in the ecosystem. Genesis 150 represents the founding generation and the original foundation of the swamp. NOX EGG represents a new generation of participants and collectors entering through the Egg Drop.

GENESIS 150

The collections may coexist in the same broader ecosystem, but they do not share the same status. The 5,000 NOX Eggs do not dilute the numerical supply of Genesis 150, and the Egg Drop does not create additional Genesis NFTs.

SEPARATE FROM THE 120-DAY POINTS SYSTEM

The Great Egg Drop is independent of the Points Program below. The Egg Drop does not shorten, reset, replace, or otherwise modify the 120-day period. Existing point accumulation and Genesis eligibility rules continue separately.

NOX EGG ownership does not automatically generate points, grant a higher ranking, or guarantee Genesis eligibility. Participation in the points system remains subject to its own rules.
